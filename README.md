### Morphological, molecular, and biological characterization of *Monalonion dissimulatum* Distant associated with cacao in Amazonas, Peru

### Resources

**Dataset:** [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BJR6o_kZH9YCPTlQtPgWaHZdYpI2c1VOJOsdPbb3bPU/edit?usp=sharing)

### Description
The study integrates field assessments, morphological characterization, DNA barcoding, and biological observations to identify *Monalonion dissimulatum* associated with cacao and characterize its damage, incidence, and life cycle under field conditions in the Amazonas region of Peru.
